Accessibility

The portfolio supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, and text zoom. Motion starts off when your device requests reduced motion, and on otherwise. Toggle Motion lets you explicitly enable or disable the Matrix background, gradient pulses, sway and bounce loops, scroll-linked entrances, and hover enlargement for the current visit. Enabling motion overrides reduced motion only for this visit; reloading restores your device's default. Device preference changes are followed until you make a manual choice. Motion-enabled projects reveal as they enter view and hide again off screen. Projects that fit the viewport briefly hold in place using native sticky positioning, without intercepting wheel or touch input. Keyboard focus reveals interactive content. With motion disabled or JavaScript unavailable, all content remains visible in ordinary document flow. Content and navigation, including the compact phone menu, remain usable without JavaScript. You can report an accessibility issue or send feedback through the portfolio issue tracker. Issues are public.
